Exploring Career Options

Personal Financial Literacy - Unit 2: Careers and Income

Beacon of Hope Christian School

Romans 8:28
Think about how your skills, talents, interests, education, and wise counsel can guide responsible career decisions.

Learning Goals

Identify resources for researching careers.
Explore and compare career options.
Analyze how education and skills affect income.
Explain how supply and demand can affect wages.

Vocabulary

Career
An occupation or profession that usually requires special training.
Demand
The desire or need for a product or service.
Income
Money received for work, products sold, rent, investments, and other sources.
Mean
An average.
Occupation
A person's usual work or business.
Salary
A fixed amount periodically paid for work.
Supply
The amount available.
Wages
Money received for work.

Practice Quiz

Career Research Challenge

Choose a career that interests you. Record its job description, education/skill requirements, job outlook, advancement opportunities, salary, location/travel expectations, and positive impact on others.

Reflection

Why should income not be the only factor when choosing a career?
